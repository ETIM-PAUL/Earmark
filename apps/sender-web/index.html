    Four steps, and only the first is yours.

    1. You fund the account and set the rules

      Add money once as USDC. Pick a preset, or set a monthly budget, a cap for each bill, and the amount above which your family has to ask you.

    2. They ask in their own words

      Mama writes "abeg buy 5k light for my meter". The assistant reads the request and finds the meter. It never touches the money.

    3. The rules decide

      Code checks your rules after she says YES, not the assistant. Inside the rules, it pays. Outside them, the request waits for you.

    4. A public receipt is recorded

      The amount, the kind of bill and the time go on NEAR. Names, phone numbers, meter numbers and tokens never do.

    Rules the assistant can't talk its way past.

    The assistant only reads the request. Your rules run as code, every time, after your family says YES.

    Hard limits
    A monthly budget and a most-per-payment amount, signed into the family account. Even a bug on our side can't go past them.
    Soft rules
    Turn each bill on or off, cap it for the month, and choose the amount above which they need your yes.
    Presets
    Start from Open, Essentials, Bills only or Strict, then change one rule at a time.

    Share it with the relatives who share the bills.

    Invite up to four relatives. Each signs in with their own passkey. Ask for two signatures on large payments and rule changes, so no one person decides alone.

    Stopping is always quick: any one relative can decline a payment or freeze the account.

    Proof anyone can check, with nothing private in it.

    A receipt page doesn't take our word for it. It reads the contract on NEAR and checks the transaction itself:

    1. It finds the transaction on NEAR.
    2. It confirms the right contract handled it.
    3. It matches our recorded event to this exact order.

    When something goes wrong, the money doesn't disappear.

    The biller fails the bill

    The money goes back to the family account, the family is told, and the receipt says Refunded.

    The biller is slow

    We check what happened before trying again, so a bill is never bought twice.

    The biller is down

    Your family is asked to try again in a minute, and nothing is charged.

    What works today

    Earmark is a hackathon build. Here is what is real and what isn't.

    Working
    Passkey sign-in, family rules and approvals, relatives co-signing, the assistant in English and Pidgin on Telegram, and public receipts from a contract on NEAR testnet.
    Simulated in the local demo
    The biller and the chain, so you can try everything with no keys and no real money.
    Not live yet
    Adding money and payments on mainnet. The Top up screen says so, rather than show a made-up balance.
