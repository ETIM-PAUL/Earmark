# For judges

Earmark lets someone abroad send money home with rules. A family member asks a chat assistant, in English or Pidgin, to pay prepaid electricity, airtime, data or TV inside those rules, and every payment gets a public receipt on NEAR with no personal data. The [README](README.md) has the full story. This page is for checking it, one claim at a time, with what you should see.

## 2. Run it, no keys, no money (10 minutes)

Node 24+ and pnpm 12 (`corepack enable`). Then `make demo` and open the link it prints: the sender's app, the recipient's chat and the chain in one page. In the chat, click **abeg buy 5k light for my meter**, then **yes**: the reply is in Pidgin, the electricity token arrives, and the chain pane shows the receipt with "✓ verified". The [README](README.md#try-it-in-10-minutes-no-keys-no-money) lists every step and what you should see.

## 4. What is not real yet

- **The money into settlement, and refunds, are simulated.** The payment references on the 63 testnet receipts are drill and demo labels, not NEAR transactions, and the privacy audit says so.
- **No mainnet transaction exists.** The receipts contract and every receipt are on testnet.
- **The bills are VTpass sandbox purchases:** the real VTpass API, with its test meter, phone and smartcard.
- **NEAR Intents** (the Agent API) is spiked and has a real-payment path in the code, off by default; it has not paid an order yet.

The README's [What is real and what is not](README.md#what-is-real-and-what-is-not) table has the details.
